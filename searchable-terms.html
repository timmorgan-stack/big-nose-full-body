Key terms

What the words mean

Vegan Fined without animal products — no isinglass, casein or egg white. Organic Grown without synthetic pesticides or herbicides, certified or practising. Biodynamic Organic, plus farming to the calendar and treating the vineyard as one organism. Unoaked Aged in steel, concrete or old neutral barrels. Fruit and site, no vanilla. Minimal Sulfur A small dose at bottling and nothing more. No Added Sulfur Nothing added at all. Alive, occasionally wild, best drunk young. Natural Native yeasts, little intervention, nothing stripped out on the way. Sustainable Farmed with a long view — water, cover crops, soil left better than found. Pét-Nat Bottled before the first fermentation finishes. Cloudy, gulpable, no two the same. Kosher Produced under rabbinical supervision.
